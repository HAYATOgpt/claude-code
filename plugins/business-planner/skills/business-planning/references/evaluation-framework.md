# RIVER Evaluation Framework - Detailed Scoring Guide

## Overview

The RIVER framework provides a structured, quantitative method to compare business ideas against each other. It removes emotional bias and forces honest assessment of each opportunity.

**RIVER** = Revenue potential, Implementation speed, Value alignment, Effort sustainability, Risk level

## Scoring Criteria (1-5 Scale)

### R - Revenue Potential (Weight: 25%)

Evaluate realistic monthly income at three time horizons.

| Score | 3-Month Revenue | 6-Month Revenue | 12-Month Revenue | Example |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|-------------------|---------|
| 1 | 0円 | 0-5,000円 | 0-1万円 | Hobby blog with no monetization strategy |
| 2 | 0-5,000円 | 5,000-2万円 | 1-3万円 | Small note sales, low-traffic content |
| 3 | 5,000-3万円 | 2-5万円 | 3-10万円 | Active freelancing, growing content |
| 4 | 1-5万円 | 5-15万円 | 10-30万円 | Established service + content funnel |
| 5 | 3-10万円 | 10-30万円 | 30万円+ | SaaS, high-value consulting, scaled education |

**Scoring notes**:
- Base estimates on comparable market data, not aspirational numbers
- Factor in the user's starting position (existing audience = higher scores)
- Consider revenue ceiling, not just initial revenue

---

### I - Implementation Speed (Weight: 20%)

How quickly the first revenue can be generated.

| Score | Time to First Revenue | Time to Sustainable Revenue | What's Needed |
|-------|----------------------|---------------------------|---------------|
| 1 | 6+ months | 12+ months | Product development, market creation |
| 2 | 3-6 months | 6-12 months | Significant learning curve, audience building |
| 3 | 1-3 months | 3-6 months | Moderate setup, existing platform |
| 4 | 2-4 weeks | 1-3 months | Existing skill, ready marketplace |
| 5 | 1-2 weeks | 1-2 months | Immediate skill match, established demand |

**Scoring notes**:
- Account for setup time (platform registration, portfolio creation, marketing)
- "First revenue" means actual money received, not just listed
- Consider the user's existing assets that reduce time-to-market

---

### V - Value Alignment (Weight: 15%)

How well the business aligns with the user's personal goals, interests, and lifestyle preferences.

| Score | Alignment Level | Indicators |
|-------|----------------|------------|
| 1 | Misaligned | Actively dislikes the work, conflicts with values |
| 2 | Tolerable | Can do it for money but finds it draining |
| 3 | Neutral | Neither exciting nor unpleasant |
| 4 | Good fit | Enjoys the work, aligns with career goals |
| 5 | Perfect fit | Passionate about it, would do it for free, aligns with long-term vision |

**Factors to assess**:
- Does this move toward or away from the user's stated long-term goals?
- Would the user enjoy doing this daily for 12+ months?
- Does it build skills/network that transfer to the user's broader career?
- Does it align with the user's lifestyle preferences (remote, flexible hours, etc.)?

---

### E - Effort Sustainability (Weight: 20%)

Can the user realistically maintain this alongside existing commitments?

| Score | Weekly Hours Required | Energy Level | Sustainability |
|-------|----------------------|-------------|----------------|
| 1 | 20+ hours | High-intensity, deadline-driven | Burns out in 1-2 months |
| 2 | 15-20 hours | Moderate-high, frequent client interactions | Difficult after 3 months |
| 3 | 10-15 hours | Moderate, self-paced with some deadlines | Sustainable for 6 months |
| 4 | 5-10 hours | Low-moderate, mostly async | Sustainable for 12+ months |
| 5 | <5 hours | Low, largely passive/automated | Indefinitely sustainable |

**Scoring notes**:
- Compare required hours against the user's stated available hours
- Factor in energy, not just time (client calls at 9pm drain more than writing)
- Consider seasonal variation (some models have demand spikes)
- Account for AI leverage (AI reduces hours but not management overhead)

---

### R - Risk Level (Weight: 20%)

Comprehensive risk assessment across multiple dimensions.

| Score | Risk Level | Financial Risk | Reputation Risk | Dependency Risk |
|-------|-----------|---------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Very high | >10万円 upfront, ongoing costs | Public failures visible | Single client/platform |
| 2 | High | 5-10万円 upfront, some ongoing | Moderate public exposure | Few clients/platforms |
| 3 | Moderate | 1-5万円 upfront, low ongoing | Limited exposure | Some diversification |
| 4 | Low | <1万円 upfront, minimal ongoing | Anonymous possible | Multiple channels |
| 5 | Very low | 0円 upfront, 0円 ongoing | No downside risk | Fully diversified |

**Risk dimensions to evaluate**:
- **Financial**: Upfront investment, ongoing costs, lost opportunity cost
- **Reputation**: Impact on current employment, public visibility of failures
- **Platform**: Dependency on single platform (algorithm changes, policy changes)
- **Client**: Dependency on few clients for revenue
- **Technology**: Risk of AI tool changes breaking the business model
- **Legal**: Employment contract restrictions, tax implications, liability

---

## Calculation Method

### Weighted Score Formula

```
Total = (R × 0.25) + (I × 0.20) + (V × 0.15) + (E × 0.20) + (R × 0.20)
```

### Comparison Table Template

| Business Idea | R (×0.25) | I (×0.20) | V (×0.15) | E (×0.20) | R (×0.20) | Total |
|---------------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Idea A        | 4 (1.00)  | 5 (1.00)  | 3 (0.45)  | 4 (0.80)  | 5 (1.00)  | 4.25  |
| Idea B        | 5 (1.25)  | 2 (0.40)  | 5 (0.75)  | 2 (0.40)  | 3 (0.60)  | 3.40  |
| Idea C        | 3 (0.75)  | 4 (0.80)  | 4 (0.60)  | 5 (1.00)  | 4 (0.80)  | 3.95  |

### Interpretation Guide

- **4.0+**: Strong recommendation. Start immediately.
- **3.5-3.9**: Good option. Consider as secondary pursuit.
- **3.0-3.4**: Viable but has significant trade-offs. Only pursue if highly motivated.
- **Below 3.0**: Not recommended given current situation. Revisit if circumstances change.

---

## Decision Matrix: Quick Win vs. High Ceiling

After scoring, categorize the top ideas:

### Quick Win (Recommend one)
- Highest **I** score (implementation speed)
- Minimum 3.0 total score
- Purpose: Generate first revenue, build confidence, learn market

### High Ceiling (Recommend one)
- Highest **R** score (revenue potential)
- Minimum 3.5 total score
- Purpose: Long-term wealth building, career transformation

### Balanced Pick (Recommend one if different from above)
- Highest total score
- Purpose: Best overall fit for the user's complete situation

---

## Adjustment Factors

Apply these multipliers based on user context:

| Factor | Condition | Adjustment |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| Existing audience | 1,000+ followers on any platform | Revenue ×1.3, Implementation ×1.2 |
| Technical skills | Can code proficiently | Revenue ×1.2 for tech products |
| Sales experience | Has sold services before | Implementation ×1.3 |
| Domain expertise | 5+ years in specific industry | Revenue ×1.5 for that domain |
| No audience | Starting from zero | Revenue ×0.7 for first 3 months |
| Limited time | <5 hours/week | Sustainability ×0.8 for time-intensive models |

---

## Sensitivity Analysis

For the top 2-3 ideas, test robustness by asking:

1. **If AI tools become 50% more expensive**, does this still work?
2. **If competition doubles in 6 months**, what's the moat?
3. **If available time drops by half**, can this still generate revenue?
4. **If the primary platform changes its policies**, is there a fallback?

Any idea that fails 2+ of these tests should be flagged as fragile.
