# AI-Powered Business Models Catalog

This reference provides a comprehensive catalog of business models that leverage AI tools (Claude Code, ChatGPT, etc.) for competitive advantage. Each model includes revenue benchmarks, required skills, and honest assessment of pros/cons.

## Category 1: Content & Media

### 1.1 Technical Blog / note Sales

**Overview**: Write in-depth articles about AI tools, development workflows, or domain-specific AI applications. Monetize through platform sales (note, Zenn), SEO traffic, or sponsorships.

- **Revenue range**: 1-10万円/月 (content volume dependent)
- **Time to first revenue**: 2-4 weeks
- **AI leverage**: 10x (AI assists research, drafting, and editing)
- **Required skills**: Writing ability, domain knowledge to validate AI output
- **Platforms**: note, Zenn, Qiita (free → traffic → paid articles)

**Pros**: Low barrier, builds personal brand, compounds over time, no client management
**Cons**: Slow revenue growth, high competition in generic AI content, requires consistency

**Differentiation strategy**: Focus on specific tool (Claude Code) or specific industry vertical rather than generic "AI tips."

---

### 1.2 Social Media Content Creation (X / Instagram / YouTube)

**Overview**: Build audience through regular AI-related content. Monetize through sponsorships, affiliate, and funnel to paid offerings.

- **Revenue range**: 0-5万円/月 (direct), unlimited (as funnel to services)
- **Time to first revenue**: 1-3 months (affiliate), 3-6 months (sponsorship)
- **AI leverage**: 5x (content ideation, script writing, image generation)
- **Required skills**: Content strategy, platform-specific format knowledge, consistency

**Platform-specific notes**:
- **X**: Best for B2B, developer audience. Thread format works well for tutorials.
- **Instagram**: Carousel/Reels for visual how-tos. Broader audience including non-technical.
- **YouTube**: Highest revenue per viewer but highest production effort.

**Pros**: Free to start, builds distribution asset, compounds, opens all other revenue streams
**Cons**: Requires 3-6 month runway before meaningful revenue, algorithm dependent

---

### 1.3 Newsletter / Subscription Content

**Overview**: Curated AI news, tutorials, or analysis delivered via email. Free tier builds audience, paid tier generates revenue.

- **Revenue range**: 3-20万円/月 (at 500+ paid subscribers)
- **Time to first revenue**: 2-3 months
- **AI leverage**: 5x (content curation, drafting, personalization)
- **Platforms**: Substack, Beehiiv, ConvertKit

**Pros**: Owns the distribution (email list), high engagement, predictable recurring revenue
**Cons**: Requires consistent publishing schedule, slow subscriber growth initially

---

## Category 2: Services & Consulting

### 2.1 AI Implementation Consulting

**Overview**: Help businesses adopt AI tools into their workflows. Assess current processes, recommend tools, train teams.

- **Revenue range**: 5-50万円/月
- **Time to first revenue**: 2-6 weeks
- **AI leverage**: 2x (AI helps with research and documentation, but human expertise is the product)
- **Required skills**: Business process understanding, multiple AI tool expertise, communication

**Service tiers**:
1. **Assessment** (5-10万円): Audit current workflow, identify AI opportunities
2. **Implementation** (10-30万円): Set up tools, create prompts, build workflows
3. **Advisory** (3-10万円/月): Ongoing support and optimization

**Pros**: High hourly rate, builds expertise through diverse clients, referral-driven
**Cons**: Time-for-money model, requires sales skills, client management overhead

---

### 2.2 AI-Augmented Development

**Overview**: Use Claude Code and AI tools to deliver software development projects at higher speed and quality.

- **Revenue range**: 10-100万円/月
- **Time to first revenue**: 1-4 weeks (if existing portfolio)
- **AI leverage**: 10x (Claude Code dramatically accelerates development)
- **Required skills**: Programming fundamentals, system design, client communication

**Service types**:
- Web application development
- API development and integration
- Automation scripts and workflows
- MVP/prototype rapid development
- Legacy code modernization

**Platforms**: Lancers, CrowdWorks, Coconala, direct referral

**Pros**: Highest revenue potential, AI creates genuine competitive advantage, scalable with subcontractors
**Cons**: Requires technical foundation, project management complexity, scope creep risk

---

### 2.3 Prompt Engineering as a Service

**Overview**: Design custom prompts, system prompts, and AI workflows for businesses.

- **Revenue range**: 3-20万円/月
- **Time to first revenue**: 1-3 weeks
- **AI leverage**: 5x (meta: using AI to design better AI usage)
- **Required skills**: Deep understanding of AI capabilities/limitations, domain knowledge

**Deliverables**: Custom prompt libraries, CLAUDE.md files, workflow documentation, training materials

**Pros**: Unique skill that's hard to self-learn, low competition, high perceived value
**Cons**: Market education needed (clients may not know they need this), value hard to quantify

---

## Category 3: Education & Training

### 3.1 Online Courses

**Overview**: Create structured learning experiences about AI tools and workflows.

- **Revenue range**: 3-30万円/月 (passive after creation)
- **Time to first revenue**: 1-2 months (creation time)
- **AI leverage**: 5x (curriculum design, content creation, exercise generation)
- **Platforms**: Udemy, ストアカ, Teachable, own site

**Course ideas**:
- "Claude Code Complete Guide: From Setup to Production"
- "AI-Driven Development for Beginners"
- "Business Process Automation with AI"
- "Prompt Engineering Masterclass"

**Pros**: Passive income after creation, establishes authority, scalable
**Cons**: High upfront time investment, requires marketing, needs updates as tools evolve

---

### 3.2 Corporate Training / Workshops

**Overview**: In-person or virtual training sessions for company teams.

- **Revenue range**: 10-50万円/session
- **Time to first revenue**: 1-3 months (sales cycle)
- **AI leverage**: 2x (materials preparation, exercise design)
- **Required skills**: Presentation, facilitation, curriculum design

**Formats**:
- Half-day workshop (3h): 10-20万円
- Full-day training: 20-40万円
- Multi-week program: 50-100万円+

**Pros**: Highest per-session revenue, builds corporate network, repeat business
**Cons**: Long sales cycle, requires confidence in presentation, scheduling constraints

---

### 3.3 1-on-1 Coaching / Mentoring

**Overview**: Personal guidance for individuals learning AI tools or building AI businesses.

- **Revenue range**: 3-15万円/月
- **Time to first revenue**: 1-2 weeks
- **AI leverage**: 2x
- **Platforms**: MENTA, ストアカ, TimeTicket, own site

**Pros**: Quick to start, high satisfaction, builds deep relationships, testimonials
**Cons**: Time-limited (can only serve few clients), scheduling challenges

---

## Category 4: Products & Tools

### 4.1 Templates & Digital Products

**Overview**: Sell reusable templates, prompt libraries, configuration files, and toolkits.

- **Revenue range**: 1-10万円/月 (passive)
- **Time to first revenue**: 1-2 weeks
- **AI leverage**: 10x (AI helps create the products)
- **Platforms**: note, Gumroad, BOOTH, own site

**Product ideas**:
- CLAUDE.md template collection (by industry/role)
- Prompt library for specific tasks (marketing, coding, analysis)
- MCP server configuration templates
- Business document template sets (AI-generated)
- Notion/spreadsheet templates for AI workflow management

**Pros**: Truly passive, low maintenance, compounds with catalog size
**Cons**: Low per-unit price, requires volume or niche targeting, easy to replicate

---

### 4.2 SaaS / Micro-SaaS

**Overview**: Build and operate small software products powered by AI.

- **Revenue range**: 5-100万円/月 (at scale)
- **Time to first revenue**: 2-4 months
- **AI leverage**: 10x (AI accelerates development, can be core of the product)
- **Required skills**: Full-stack development, product design, marketing

**Ideas**:
- AI-powered report generator for specific industry
- Automated content repurposing tool
- AI code review service
- Domain-specific chatbot builder

**Pros**: Recurring revenue, scalable, builds valuable asset
**Cons**: Highest complexity, requires ongoing maintenance, customer support

---

### 4.3 Automation-as-a-Service

**Overview**: Build and maintain automated workflows for clients using AI + no-code/low-code tools.

- **Revenue range**: 5-30万円/月 (recurring maintenance contracts)
- **Time to first revenue**: 2-4 weeks
- **AI leverage**: 10x
- **Tools**: Claude Code, Make (Integromat), Zapier, n8n, custom scripts

**Examples**:
- Automated email response systems
- Data extraction and reporting pipelines
- Social media scheduling and content generation
- Invoice processing automation
- Customer support triage

**Pros**: Recurring revenue from maintenance, high perceived value, demonstrable ROI
**Cons**: Client dependency, maintenance burden, needs reliability guarantees

---

## Category 5: Community & Platform

### 5.1 Paid Community

**Overview**: Create a gated community for AI practitioners to share knowledge, get support, and network.

- **Revenue range**: 5-50万円/月 (at 50-500 members)
- **Time to first revenue**: 2-3 months (need audience first)
- **AI leverage**: 2x (content creation, moderation assistance)
- **Platforms**: Discord, Slack, Circle, own platform

**Membership tiers**:
- Basic (980円/月): Community access, monthly Q&A
- Pro (2,980円/月): + exclusive content, priority support
- Premium (9,800円/月): + 1-on-1 monthly call, early access

**Pros**: Recurring revenue, network effects, creates ecosystem for upselling
**Cons**: Requires existing audience, heavy community management, churn risk

## Revenue Stacking Strategy

The most successful approach combines models across categories:

```
Content (free)
  → builds audience
    → sells courses (passive income)
    → attracts consulting clients (high income)
    → grows community (recurring income)
      → creates upsell for premium services
```

### Recommended Combinations by Profile

**Profile A: Engineer with limited time (5-10h/week)**
1. X content (daily, 15min) → 2. Coconala development services → 3. note technical articles

**Profile B: Non-engineer, good communication (10-20h/week)**
1. X/Instagram content → 2. AI consulting for SMBs → 3. Online course → 4. Community

**Profile C: Full commitment (20h+/week)**
1. Content across platforms → 2. Development services → 3. SaaS product → 4. Training + Community

**Profile D: Student/Career changer**
1. Learning in public (X/note) → 2. Templates/digital products → 3. Coaching → 4. Freelance services
